### Selected Sentinel-1 product

The project uses Sentinel-1 Level-1 IW SLC products.

For IW SLC:

- range resolution: approximately 2.7–3.5 m
- azimuth resolution: approximately 22 m
- range pixel spacing: approximately 2.3 m
- azimuth pixel spacing: approximately 14.1 m
- number of looks: 1 × 1
- ENL: 1

SLC products preserve the complex SAR signal required for
interferometric phase analysis.

Pixel spacing and spatial resolution are not equivalent. Pixel
spacing describes the sampling interval, whereas spatial resolution
describes the ability of the SAR system to distinguish nearby targets.
